Pickleball against pensioners, tech bros, aliens and a sea monster. Bring an anvil.

Pickled is a single-player arcade pickleball roguelite. Climb from the local park to the world final, one terrible idea at a time. Coming to Steam, iOS and Android.

Watch the trailer

Everyone plays pickleball now

Pensioners. Tech bros. A dog who buries the ball. A retired dentist who decides what's in. Aliens who want to be taken to your dinker, raptors, robots on nine percent battery, knights on horseback, and a sea monster who brought two paddles. They all play their own way, and they all have something to say about yours.

So bring an anvil

Serve one. It was in your bag the whole time. Or set the ball on fire, play with an actual pickle, drop a piano on someone, or throw your paddle, having first checked where your partner is. The umpire will allow it. The umpire allows all of it.

You have three knees

Every lost match costs one, and you pick the route: matches, shops, rest stops and the occasional shady deal. A man in a visor will pay cash for your good knee. He has brought a cooler.

The sea monster is the easy bit

Doreen will hit your anvil back. When your knees have gone the run is over, but what you've unlocked stays, and she's making you a little hat, for after. One more game?

Every good tune needs one wrong note.
